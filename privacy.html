Good To Know

Privacy & Cookies

A plain-language rundown of what this site does and doesn't do with your data.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to see which pages get visited and roughly how people find us — that's it. Google Analytics only runs if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner; if you click "Decline" (or just ignore it), no analytics script loads and no analytics cookies are set. Google Analytics may see your IP address and general location, but this site doesn't hand it your name, phone number, or anything else identifying.

Ordering

Orders placed through this site happen entirely over WhatsApp — clicking "Order via WhatsApp" opens a chat with your order pre-filled as a message. That conversation lives in WhatsApp, not on this website; we don't store your order, phone number, or any other order details in a database here.

Your Cart

What's in your cart while you're browsing the menu is kept only in your own browser (not sent anywhere) until you choose to send it via WhatsApp.

Your Cookie Choice

Whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies is remembered in your browser only, so the banner doesn't ask you every visit. You can reset this — and clear the choice — by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Questions

Email Burgersnbeyondsa@gmail.com if you have any questions about this.
